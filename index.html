I’m a PhD student in artificial intelligence at Fudan University, working with Pujiang Laboratory on embodied AI. I study what a robot should remember from its past and how that memory can guide its next action.

I studied robotics at Peking University, where I used molecular dynamics to explore how bending a boron arsenide nanoribbon changes the way it carries heat. Our findings appeared in Physical Review Applied.

My recent work, VOW, studies how robots can improve their visuomotor policies from successful and failed attempts. Outside the lab, I enjoy baseball, sanda, and traditional Chinese arts.
